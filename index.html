GET TO KNOW ME

What drives you as a creator?
The moment something changes — when a complex problem resolves into something curious and simple. That invisible work is what I live for.
What do you do outside work?
You'll find me out running, listening to music on full blast, hunting for the perfect photo, or rewatching a favourite film. I believe a rich life outside work makes for better work.
Skill vs talent — what's your superpower?
Never giving up. If i don't know something, I put all effort in learning every detail of the subject. I haven't unlocked a talent in a specific activity, but I'm convinced that my talent is dedication
If you could go anywhere right now?
Somewhere breathtaking — a city I've never visited before, mountain routes I've never walked. My camera and headphones are always packed.
What are you obsessed with lately?
Generative art with Python, cinematic colour grading in LightRoom, and exploring the Computer Science.
